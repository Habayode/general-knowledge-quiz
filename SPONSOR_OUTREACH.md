# gkall · Sponsor Outreach (10 first-wave targets)

**Goal:** 1–2 sponsors signed (≥$50 USDT each) before public launch on June 1, 2026.

**Why now:** A funded sponsor wallet visible on the Sponsor page makes the May-pool guarantee feel more solid for Pioneers, and "X is a Sponsor" is an artifact we can post on launch day.

**Approach:** Personal email from `hello@gkall.online`. Never spray-and-pray; each draft below is hand-tailored to the recipient. **Send max 3 per day** so the operator can actually engage with replies.

**Acceptance rate to expect:** 1–2 in 10. The point of casting 10 is to find those 1–2.

---

## How to use this file

Each draft below has:
- 🎯 **Why this brand** — the angle that makes you relevant to them
- 📧 **To** — public email or contact form
- 🪧 **Subject** — under 60 chars, no clickbait
- ✉️ **Body** — copy/paste, swap `[YOUR NAME]` for the operator name

Send via Gmail with the `hello@gkall.online` reply-to. Track responses in a single Notes file (date sent, opened, replied, outcome).

## What "yes" looks like

Most likely shape of an accepted sponsorship:
- Reply within 48 hours of send
- Ask for a 15-min call OR a one-pager
- After call: send wallet send to `TCUQq4iHrAW4vzAE97ZHNFPHeoqBh5XarM`
- Email `hello@gkall.online` with the tx hash + how they'd like to be credited
- Sponsor goes live on the Sponsor Wall within 24 hours of confirmation

## What "no" looks like (and how to handle)

- No reply in 7 days → assume no. Don't follow up more than once.
- "Not the right fit right now" → ask if there's a referral. Reply with "thanks, would there be a better team to introduce us to?" 50% give one.
