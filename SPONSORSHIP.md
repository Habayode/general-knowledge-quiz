## Why sponsor gkall

- **Tax-effective marketing.** Funded prize money goes to real people who win it. Your brand appears on every winner announcement, every monthly leaderboard, and the Sponsor wall.

- **Pioneer audience.** Launching with engaged Pioneer testers and growing daily.

## Common objections & responses

**"How do I know the money actually goes to winners?"**
→ Every prize payout is a Tronscan link, public on the Past Winners page. You can audit every dollar.

**"What if no one plays in the month I sponsor?"**
→ Prize rolls over to the next month. You still get the recognition for the month you sponsored.

**"Can I sponsor a specific category?"**
→ Yes — Title Sponsor tier. "Brilliant.org-sponsored Math Challenge" is the kind of branded category we can run.

**"What's the player demographic?"**
→ Currently early Pioneers, crypto-aware. Diversifies post-launch as we promote to trivia + education communities.

**"Why not Twitch / YouTube / TikTok directly?"**
→ Those are great. We're complementary — a destination for engaged knowledge fans, not entertainment passive viewing.

## Long-term sponsorship sustainability

The math (post-prelaunch reset to right-sized prizes):
- $85/month base prize pool + ~$1 × 10–30 winners/month ≈ $100–115/month
- We need ~$150/month in sponsorship to cover prizes + buffer
- At Pioneer Sponsor tier ($100 avg), that's 1–2 Pioneer Sponsors per month — entirely sustainable
- At Title Sponsor tier ($1,500 avg), that's 1 Title Sponsor every ~10 months — funds the whole pool for 10 months
- **Realistic mix:** 1 Round + 1 Pioneer per month = sustainable headroom

When sponsorship exceeds $500/month, we scale up:
- Restore monthly prize pool to $225 (top 3: $100/$75/$50) like our original launch plan
- Add a $5 weekly prize (top 3 of the week)
- Increase instant prize from $1 → $5 per 10/10
- Fund question-pool growth via paid content licensing
